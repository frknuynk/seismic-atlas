# Product-owner research basis

## Evidence

- The [official Scrum Guide](https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html) defines a product as a value vehicle with a clear boundary, known stakeholders, and well-defined users; the Product Owner is accountable for maximizing value and making the Product Goal and backlog transparent.
- The [AFAD Event Web Service](https://deprem.afad.gov.tr/event-service) exposes time, geography, depth, magnitude, ordering, offset, and event-ID contracts. Seismic Atlas narrows that upstream capability to an AFAD-only rolling seven-day product.
- [AFAD's earthquake portal](https://deprem.afad.gov.tr/) states that official basic earthquake data is announced by the Presidency. The product therefore needs unambiguous attribution and independent-product language.

## Project-specific interpretation

The owner audit prioritizes the map-first recent-seismicity job, transparent data health, and honest scientific boundaries. A feature that works technically but contradicts AFAD-only, seven-day, no-export, non-warning, or non-forecast positioning is a release defect. The decision must consider the whole journey on desktop and mobile, not feature count.
