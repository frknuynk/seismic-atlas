# Earthquake-enthusiast research basis

## Evidence

- The [USGS Latest Earthquakes map help](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/map/?about=true) documents a public interaction model where marker size represents magnitude, color represents age, and selecting a marker synchronizes map and list context.
- [AFAD explains magnitude versus intensity](https://www.afad.gov.tr/kurumlar/afad.gov.tr/39500/xfiles/deprem_nedir.pdf): magnitude characterizes energy at the source, while intensity describes observed effects and damage. Public-facing language must not blur them.
- [USGS explains why agency magnitudes differ](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/why-do-usgs-earthquake-magnitudes-differ-those-published-other-agencies) and notes that revisions and differences of a few tenths can be normal outcomes of method and added data.
- WCAG 2.2 guidance covers [keyboard navigation and predictable focus](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/quickref/), [visible/unobscured focus](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/new-in-22/), and [programmatic status messages](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/status-messages.html).

## Project-specific interpretation

The non-specialist needs rapid orientation, not a seismology lesson. Technical values should remain available but be paired with units, source, time context, freshness, and caveats. The interface must never be mistaken for an alert, official AFAD product, hazard estimate, felt-intensity report, or prediction.
